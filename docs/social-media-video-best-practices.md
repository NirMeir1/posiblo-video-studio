# Social media marketing video: research and production guide

Research date: **2026-11-15**. Platforms: Facebook, Instagram, TikTok. Scope: organic marketing videos and paid short-form video ads, with practical application to Posiblo's business education videos.

Companion: [reusable research prompt](prompts/social-media-video-research-prompt.md).

## 1. How to use this report

The most useful starting point is a video that answers one audience problem, demonstrates something credible, and gives the viewer one clear next action. Build for the intended placement, check readability on a phone, and evaluate business results alongside attention.

Evidence labels used below:

- **Platform requirement/specification:** stated in official documentation; scope matters.
- **Platform recommendation:** the platform's suggested approach, not a guarantee.
- **Accessibility guidance:** W3C guidance, separate from platform upload requirements.
- **Posiblo recommendation/test:** an editorial or measurement proposal for this repository, not an established platform rule.
- **Unverified:** could not be established from accessible primary evidence during this research.

Only official platform publications and W3C support the external findings. Search surfaced agency articles and forum discussions, but those were not used as evidence. Platform marketing research can be useful while still favoring the platform's products. No causal performance uplift is promised here. No account analytics, campaign results, videos, audio, or platform previews were inspected for this report.

Some official sources are older announcements. Their dates are included because announcements do not prove that every feature is currently available to every account. Recheck live publishing controls and ad specifications before delivery.

## 2. Decisions to make before editing

**Posiblo recommendation:** record these in the [video brief](video-brief-template.md):

| Decision | What to record | Why it changes production |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Viewer | Business type, language, problem, existing knowledge | Determines vocabulary and examples |
| Main message | One useful sentence | Prevents unrelated explanations |
| Goal | Awareness, education, qualified inquiry, booking, sale | Determines proof and CTA |
| Distribution | Organic or paid; specific app and placement | Determines layout, upload checks, and measurement |
| Proof | Actual demonstration, sourced fact, approved customer result | Determines what can truthfully be shown |
| Action | One feasible action and destination | Makes the closing useful |
| Success | Primary metric and measurement window | Prevents choosing winners by views alone |
| Variants | Hypothesis and exact difference | Makes later feedback actionable |

An educational organic post can succeed by answering a question and earning relevant follow-up interest. A lead-generation ad must also deliver the promised next step. Do not equate an engaging video with a profitable campaign.

## 3. Findings by platform

### Facebook: original work, relevant packaging, separate ad checks

**Platform guidance:** Meta's March 2026 originality clarification prioritizes original material in Feed and Reels. Creator-produced footage qualifies; selected third-party footage needs substantial new information, analysis, or storytelling. Reuploads with borders, captions, speed changes, or superficial reactions can be deprioritized. This is distribution guidance; it does not grant copyright permission. [Meta: rewarding original creators](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/rewarding-original-creators-on-facebook/).

**Platform guidance:** Meta identifies long, distracting post captions with excessive hashtags, unrelated captions, and coordinated spam as reach/monetization problems. This is not a blanket ban on useful long explanations. Here, “caption” means the post description, not accessibility subtitles. [Meta: cracking down on spammy content](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/04/cracking-down-spammy-content-facebook/).

**Platform announcement:** in June 2025, Meta announced that new Facebook video publishing would transition to Reels, including videos of different lengths and formats. It also highlighted audience controls and public sharing for discovery. Do not apply old organic Reels duration rules automatically, or infer paid-ad acceptance from this organic publishing announcement. [Meta: easier video creation](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/06/making-it-easier-create-videos-facebook/).

**Posiblo recommendations:** use your own speaker and demonstrations; explain an actual business workflow; make the post description match the video; check intended audience visibility. For ads, inspect each selected placement separately. Consider a separately composed 4:5 Feed version if appropriate, but treat that as a design proposal until the live Ads Guide and account preview confirm suitability.

### Instagram: original material and account-specific learning

**Platform guidance, translated from Meta's Portuguese publication:** Meta's April 2024 announcement describes original, eligible content without visible watermarks as suitable for recommendations. It distinguishes recommendation eligibility from permission to remain on the platform and explains replacing identical reposts with originals in recommendation surfaces. This is historical guidance, not a complete reconstruction of today's ranking system. [Meta: helping creators reach new audiences](https://about.fb.com/br/news/2024/04/ajudando-o-criador-de-conteudo-a-encontrar-novos-publicos/).

**Platform recommendation:** Instagram's professional dashboard includes Best Practices covering creation, engagement, reach, monetization, and guidelines, including personalized tips. Review the account's current guidance rather than imposing a universal posting frequency, Reel length, or hashtag count. [Meta: Instagram Best Practices hub](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/10/best-practices-education-hub-creators-instagram/).

**Platform feature:** Trial Reels can introduce a video to non-followers first. The announcement describes initial insights around 24 hours and an optional automatic sharing decision based on the first 72 hours. Meta later announced broader availability in June 2025. Check whether the control is available for the intended account. Trial distribution is not a randomized A/B experiment, and some followers may encounter a trial through shares. [Trial Reels explanation](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/12/trial-reels-try-content-non-followers-first-see-what-perfoms-best/), [June 2025 update](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/06/inspiring-creativity-that-brings-people-together/).

**Posiblo recommendations:** export a clean master without another app's watermark; retain Posiblo branding. Test an explanation people would want to save or send to a colleague. Prepare a readable cover, then check its actual profile and feed crops. Use available account insights to judge reach, retention, and useful actions separately.

### TikTok: native presentation, early value, meaningful variation

**Platform recommendations:** TikTok's performance guide favors people, a natural presentation, 9:16 framing, sound, at least 720p production quality, and interface-safe composition. It recommends communicating the proposition within three seconds and prioritizing the hook within six. These are starting points for advertising, not guaranteed organic ranking rules. The same guide suggests distinct creative variants and refreshing assets when results show sustained decline. [TikTok: creative best practices](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/creative-best-practices).

**Platform recommendations:** Creative Codes describes six principles: platform-native content, relevant trends, production fundamentals, a hook/body/close structure, attention-supporting editing, and sound. Its sound-related research claim cites US research from January 2021; this report does not generalize that claim to all audiences or claim TikTok uniquely benefits from audio today. [TikTok: Creative Codes](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/creative-codes).

**Platform feature:** Creator Search Insights surfaces searched topics and content gaps, with regional availability noted in its launch publication. Use it to investigate real audience questions if available; it does not guarantee discovery. [TikTok: Creator Search Insights](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/creator-search-insights?lang=en).

**Posiblo recommendations:** test a direct explanation from the real speaker alongside a workflow demonstration. Match trends only when they clarify the topic and rights allow use. A useful question such as how to move a lead into a tracking system is a better research starting point than a generic “AI” theme. Keep the approved Posiblo design; test fewer simultaneous graphics rather than abandoning visual quality.

## 4. Creative instructions from opening to closing

The following is a **Posiblo editorial framework to test**, informed by the platform recommendations above. Its exact sequence and timing are not platform requirements.

1. **Opening:** show the business problem, an interesting result, or a relevant demonstration immediately. Remove empty introductions only when an authorized edit preserves meaning and continuity.
2. **Promise:** make the viewer's benefit specific and supportable. Avoid unexplained jargon and unsupported savings claims.
3. **Explanation:** show one process or example. Reveal diagram elements when the speaker introduces them.
4. **Evidence:** show what actually happened, or label a hypothetical example clearly. Identify material conditions behind customer results.
5. **Closing:** ask for one action appropriate to the goal and available interface. A reply, message, profile visit, booking, and purchase are different commitments.

TikTok's small-business guide supports early attention, captions, benefit-focused communication, mobile-friendly composition, and problem/solution/benefit storytelling. It also recommends 1080 × 1920 or higher for crisp vertical playback. Its advice is useful as a starting point across the production process, but it is TikTok guidance. [TikTok: video advertising guide](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/guides/video-advertising-guide).

### Length and pacing

**Unverified:** one best length across Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok. Upload limits, recommendation eligibility, and an effective creative length are different questions.

**Posiblo test:** where the actual speech supports it, compare a concise 15–25 second explanation with a 30–45 second version. These ranges are experiment choices, not researched optimums. Compare qualified actions and retention within each duration group. Do not cut a necessary explanation just to reach a target or compare completion rates without considering length.

Use visual changes when the information changes. Constant transitions, forced loops, and arbitrary cuts are not established requirements. Preserve enough time to read the graphic and caption together.

### Verified TikTok paid In-Feed specifications

The official page was last updated June 2026. These values apply to the named formats, not all TikTok products. [TikTok: Auction In-Feed Ads](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/tiktok-auction-in-feed-ads).

| Item | Non-Spark In-Feed | Spark Ads (Pull) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vertical dimensions | Recommended 9:16; minimum 540 × 960 | Consult source post and live workflow |
| Other dimensions | 16:9 minimum 960 × 540; 1:1 minimum 640 × 640 | Consult live workflow |
| Duration | Up to 10 minutes | Page lists no duration restrictions for this format |
| File size | At most 500 MB | Not established here |
| Bitrate | At least 516 kbps | Not established here |
| File formats | MP4, MOV, MPEG, 3GP, AVI | MP4, MOV |

The page supplies safe-zone templates based on dimensions, ad-description length, region, and additional formats such as anchors. It warns previews can differ from live rendering. Its Arabic RTL template is regional: Hebrew caption direction alone is not a reason to select it. Follow the intended region/format instructions.

### Posiblo production settings

These come from the repository's [video standard](video-standard.md), not a claim that all platforms mandate them:

- Default vertical H.264 MP4, 30 fps, AAC 192 kbps, yuv420p, CRF 18.
- Preserve the 720 × 1280 baseline minimum; prefer 1080 × 1920 when source quality supports it.
- A higher export resolution does not recover missing source detail.
- Keep a clean master and create distinct filenames for placement variants.
- Compose a Feed crop deliberately if needed; do not assume automatic cropping preserves the speaker, diagram, and captions.

**Safe-zone procedure:** preview the actual video with the intended post/ad description, CTA, and platform controls. Inspect the opening, densest diagram, longest caption, and closing. Check a phone-sized view, including mixed Hebrew/Latin text. Record screenshots and device/app context when performing that review. A single bottom margin is not proof of cross-platform safety.

## 6. Captions, audio, accessibility, and Hebrew

**Accessibility guidance:** W3C explains that captions include meaningful speech and relevant non-speech audio. Automatic captions are only a starting point unless confirmed accurate. Missing a negation can reverse meaning. [W3C: captions/subtitles](https://www.w3.org/WAI/media/av/captions/).

**Posiblo instructions:**

- Have a fluent Hebrew listener compare the complete captions with the final audio; record reviewer, date, and unresolved words.
- Verify RTL punctuation, numerals, Latin product names, and URLs in the rendered output.
- Group words by meaning and reading comfort. Keep a stable, high-contrast backing and avoid covering the speaker or proof.
- Use the current active-word highlight only when it supports comprehension. Do not rely on color alone for meaning.
- Inspect transitions for captions cut off by scene changes and accidental double captions from native subtitles plus embedded text.
- Listen for clipping, distracting noise, abrupt edits, music masking speech, and audio/video drift.
- When a diagram carries essential information, explain it in speech or provide a suitable text alternative. Plan accessibility before editing. [W3C: planning accessible media](https://www.w3.org/WAI/media/av/planning/).

TikTok's performance guide suggests 5–10 displayed words per second for text overlays. That recommendation is not proof that fast Hebrew subtitles are readable or accessible. Prioritize actual comprehension and the existing Posiblo caption standard over mechanically applying that number.

## 7. Commercial content, music, proof, and destination

**TikTok requirement:** promotional content includes marketing your own business and incentivized third-party promotion. Turn on content disclosure and select the correct relationship; TikTok distinguishes Promotional content from Paid partnership. Its help page says enabling disclosure does not affect feed distribution. [TikTok: promoting a brand, product, or service](https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/creator-and-business-accounts/promoting-a-brand-product-or-service).

**TikTok music guidance:** use its Commercial Music Library for promotional content, or obtain the necessary rights for outside music. Availability in the consumer library does not establish commercial rights. [TikTok: commercial use of music](https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/creator-and-business-accounts/commercial-use-of-music-on-tiktok).

**TikTok ad policy:** ads and landing pages must not promise or exaggerate product results; misleading visual tactics are also prohibited. Verify the particular claims and creative against the applicable policy. [TikTok: misleading and false content](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/tiktok-ads-policy-misleading-and-false-content).

**Posiblo review procedure:** maintain a rights record for footage, customer material, music, and paid usage. Check rights separately for each destination platform; do not assume a TikTok-cleared track can travel to Meta. For Meta, verify the current commercial music and partnership-disclosure terms in the official account tools; the relevant music help page could not be read here. This report does not establish jurisdiction-specific legal compliance.

Before running a conversion ad, test its actual action: does the message open, booking work, or page load? Does the destination deliver the same offer and conditions? Use labeled sample data in demonstrations and remove exposed customer information. Treat these as production checks, not claims about checks already completed.

## 8. Measurement: judge the goal and understand definitions

**Posiblo measurement plan:**

| Goal | Primary result to define | Useful diagnostics | Interpretation limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Awareness | Relevant reach; lift where measurable | Early retention, watch time, frequency | Views alone do not establish recall |
| Education | Useful engagement from intended audience | Saves, shares, completion, substantive questions | Engagement is not revenue |
| Lead generation | Qualified inquiries/bookings; cost per qualified lead for paid | Clicks, landing visits, lead completion | Cheap leads can have low quality |
| Sales | Verified purchases and acquisition economics | Checkout conversion, reported ROAS | Attribution is not proof of incrementality |

Use consistent definitions: outbound CTR = outbound link clicks / impressions; qualified-lead cost = spend / qualified leads; ROAS = attributed revenue / spend. Specify the attribution window and what makes a lead qualified. Handle zero denominators as undefined rather than reporting a misleading zero.

**Metric warning:** TikTok's general six-second video-view metric can include a qualifying engagement within six seconds, rather than six full seconds watched. Average play time can include replays. Focused-view products have additional distinctions. Preserve the exact exported metric names and definitions; do not call every six-second metric pure retention. [TikTok: video play metrics](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/video-play?lang=en).

Do not compare raw platform “views” as if definitions were identical. Compare organic and paid separately, and record platform, placement, audience, spend, duration, exposure period, and metric denominator. Validate measurement before blaming the edit for low conversions.

## 9. Testing plan and feedback loop

**Platform feature:** TikTok split testing chooses results using the initially selected key metric and can report either a winner or no winner at a 90% confidence level. Its documentation also notes delayed conversion reporting for some iOS campaigns. No winner is a legitimate outcome. [TikTok: split-test results](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/split-test-results).

**Posiblo experiment procedure:**

1. Establish the goal, current baseline, primary metric, observation period, and practical decision threshold before launching.
2. Start with distinct concepts for exploration. Then isolate an important variable when trying to explain why one concept works.
3. For a controlled comparison, keep audience, offer, destination, budget conditions, and duration consistent where feasible; use platform experiment tools when available.
4. Separate organic observations and Trial Reels from controlled paid experiments. Algorithmic delivery can expose variants to different people.
5. Record uncertainty and conversion delay. Avoid declaring victory from a handful of leads or one day's volatility.
6. Keep the useful element, make the next specific revision, and record the result. Refresh based on sustained evidence, not a fixed calendar alone.

| Test proposal | Difference | Expected signal | Guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | Business problem vs. demonstration first | Better early retention | Qualified inquiries remain relevant |
| Explanation | One workflow vs. several examples | Better comprehension/watch-through | Necessary context preserved |
| Presentation | Speaker-led vs. diagram-led | Better retention through explanation | Brand and caption clarity preserved |
| Length | Concise vs. fuller explanation | Better goal result per exposure/spend | Compare within length groups too |
| CTA | One specific next step vs. current closing | More qualified actions | Offer and destination identical |

These are hypotheses, not measured Posiblo findings. No campaign was run.

## 10. Actionable feedback rubric

Use **observation → evidence → proposed change → verification**. Tie feedback to the actual file and timestamp. Separate reviewer preference from observed failure and measured performance.

| Observed symptom | Possible explanation, not a diagnosis | Revision to test | Evidence to inspect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Early audience loss | Unclear opening or audience mismatch | Show value sooner; name the problem | Opening frames, retention, audience |
| Loss during explanation | Dense diagram or unnecessary detour | Reveal fewer elements at a time | Timestamp and phone-sized playback |
| High completion, few actions | Weak offer, CTA, or destination | Clarify action and test destination | Funnel data and qualified actions |
| High clicks, weak lead quality | Promise attracts wrong audience | Clarify who benefits and conditions | Lead review and source matching |
| Caption hidden | Platform UI collision | Reposition and recompose | Actual placement preview |
| Speech hard to follow | Noise, music, or rapid captions | Improve mix and caption grouping | Full listening and caption review |
| Performance declines | Fatigue, auction shift, audience, tracking | Diagnose context; test fresh concept | Delivery and conversion trends |

Example feedback format, **illustrative only**: “At [timestamp], the caption overlaps [control] in [placement]. Move it into the verified safe area and recheck the full sentence on a phone.” Do not fill in observations until someone performs the check.

## 11. Posiblo priorities and release checklist

This report supplements the approved [video standard](video-standard.md); it does not revise the standard or approve a new visual identity.

**First priorities for the next video:**

1. Audit caption placement in actual target-platform previews. The standard's 108 px baseline offset at 720 × 1280 is a starting value, not platform-safety evidence.
2. Test a more immediately useful opening using the actual recording; do not invent speech.
3. Make the diagram explain one concrete process at the moment the speaker discusses it.
4. Complete Hebrew caption review and document any unresolved phrase. The approved reference's known omitted clause is not a transcript template for new work.
5. Measure qualified interest alongside attention. Document one improvement and its evidence for every new video.

These priorities follow the repository documentation and research; they are not findings from watching the approved MP4.

### Before publishing or running an ad

- [ ] Completed brief names audience, message, action, placement, hypothesis, and exact output path.
- [ ] Claims, examples, permissions, music rights, and applicable disclosure controls checked.
- [ ] Current placement specifications verified; blocked Meta details resolved in official tools.
- [ ] Hook, diagram, captions, speaker, brand, and CTA inspected at phone size with platform UI.
- [ ] Final export watched completely with sound; Hebrew captions checked against audio by a fluent listener.
- [ ] Destination/action tested; measurement definitions and attribution recorded.
- [ ] Automated checks run against the actual deliverable path.
- [ ] [Review record](video-review-template.md) contains reviewer, date, timestamps, improvement, unresolved issues, and human release decision.
- [ ] Approved archive preserved; new composition and distinct output filenames used.

For future exports, the existing project command is:

```powershell
npm run verify:video -- --output out/<slug>.mp4 --source public/input/<source>.mp4 --captions src/<captions>.json
```

This report did not run video checks because it creates documentation only. Automated decoding/metadata/caption checks cannot certify creative quality, caption meaning, or perceptual lip sync.

## 12. What the evidence does not establish

- A universal best posting time, posting frequency, hashtag count, video length, hook rate, or conversion benchmark.
- That a trending sound or hashtag causes improved performance.
- That paid-ad recommendations automatically describe organic ranking.
- That a visible competitor ad is profitable, or that views demonstrate incremental sales.
- That platform-reported research transfers unchanged to Hebrew business education in Israel.
- Current numeric Meta ad limits and safe-zone margins behind the blocked pages.
- Current account eligibility for every feature, or music rights across platforms and regions.

For these questions, **I do not know** the account-specific answer. Use current official controls and measured tests rather than presenting assumptions as facts.
