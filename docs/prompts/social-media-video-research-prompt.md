# Research prompt: Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok marketing video

Use this prompt to refresh the [research report](../social-media-video-best-practices.md).

```text
Act as a rigorous social-video researcher and practical creative reviewer.
Research current best practices for marketing videos on Facebook, Instagram,
and TikTok. Go beyond generic tips and produce a source-backed production,
publishing, testing, and feedback guide in English.

Context:
- Repository: Posiblo video studio.
- Audience: business owners; validate the particular audience in each brief.
- Content: real-speaker explanations, business workflows, Hebrew captions,
  and topic-specific motion graphics.
- Read AGENTS.md, docs/video-standard.md, docs/video-brief-template.md,
  docs/video-review-template.md, and any existing research report first.
- This task is research/documentation. Do not create, edit, export, publish,
  or buy distribution for a video, or alter the approved reference archive.

Source discipline:
1. Browse current official Meta, Instagram, and TikTok documentation,
   creator resources, policy pages, technical guides, and dated announcements.
   Use W3C for accessibility. Prefer primary evidence throughout.
2. Read source content, not just search snippets. Record publication/update
   date when visible and the actual research/access date.
3. If primary guidance is in another language, identify translation and scope.
4. Separate hard requirements, platform recommendations, accessibility
   guidance, research findings, and your own proposed experiments.
5. Distinguish organic posts from ads and each placement/ad format. Separate
   upload acceptance limits from recommended production settings and creative
   performance advice. Check region, objective, and account dependencies.
6. For numerical performance claims, inspect sample, period, market, comparison,
   uncertainty, and sponsorship. Omit unsupported numbers. Do not turn platform
   case studies into guaranteed results or causal laws.
7. If a page is blocked, login-only, unavailable, or contradictory, say exactly
   what was inaccessible or unresolved. Do not cite it as verified evidence.
8. Do not rely on agency blogs, SEO summaries, anecdotes, or forum comments
   for factual recommendations. Never invent citations or account results.
9. Link directly to supporting pages near each finding, and provide a source
   register with evidence role, date, and access status. Paraphrase concisely.

Investigate:
- Audience, objective, offer, funnel stage, proof, and one clear CTA.
- Hooks, first-frame value, branding, storytelling, demonstrations,
  authenticity, pacing, duration choices, and appropriate motion graphics.
- Platform-specific organic discovery, originality, recommendation eligibility,
  watermarks, search/topic tools, post descriptions, hashtags, covers,
  frequency, and timing. State where universal answers are not established.
- Reels, Stories, Feed, TikTok In-Feed, Spark versus Non-Spark, and applicable
  technical specs: ratio, resolution, frame rate, format/codec, duration,
  file size, bitrate, crops, UI safe zones, and placement previews.
- Captions, meaningful sound, mute comprehension, music/voice balance,
  accessibility, Hebrew RTL, mixed-language rendering, and human review.
- Commercial music rights, promotional/branded-content disclosure,
  creator permissions, truthful claims, and destination consistency.
  Flag applicable policy checks without claiming a legal compliance audit.
- Metrics and their exact definitions, attribution limits, qualified leads,
  retention, completion, CPA/ROAS, controlled tests, creative fatigue,
  and feedback from real audience questions.

Deliverables:
1. Write docs/social-media-video-best-practices.md with an actionable overview,
   platform comparisons, verified specification table, source-backed findings,
   detailed production instructions, release checklist, and source register.
2. Include a measurement/test plan and a symptom-to-revision feedback rubric.
   Require observation, file/timestamp, evidence, proposed change, verification,
   reviewer, and unresolved issues. Distinguish illustrative feedback from
   actual video inspection and campaign results.
3. Provide Posiblo-specific priorities while preserving the existing standard,
   real speech, distinct outputs, archive, and documented improvement rule.
4. Clearly list unknowns, access limitations, and remaining account-level checks.
   Never claim listening, caption review, visual inspection, or release readiness
   unless those checks were actually performed and recorded.
5. Keep this reusable prompt in docs/prompts/social-media-video-research-prompt.md.
6. Review Markdown, local links, source support, date/scope consistency, and the
   changed-file list. Report the paths created and material unresolved issues.

Write plainly and precisely. Where reliable evidence is unavailable, say
"I do not know" and explain how the answer could be verified.
```
